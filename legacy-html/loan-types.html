Loan Type 01

Merchant Cash Advance Loans

A merchant cash advance was originally structured as a lump sum payment to a business in exchange for an agreed-upon percentage of its future credit and/or debit card sales. Today, the term is also used to describe purchases of future card sales receivables, as well as short-term business loans.

How it works

  1. 1 You receive a lump sum of working capital up front.
  2. 2 A set percentage of your daily card sales goes toward repayment.
  3. 3 When sales are slower, your payments are smaller too.
Best for

Retailers, restaurants, salons and other businesses with steady credit and debit card sales.

Loan Type 02

Unsecured Working Capital Loans

An unsecured loan is a loan that is not backed by collateral. It's the opposite of a secured loan, which is backed by a property or asset. Because nothing is pledged, the lender can't seize your assets if you default — which also means unsecured loans typically carry a higher interest rate, as the lender takes on more risk.

Unsecured loan
  • No collateral required
  • Assets can't be seized
  • Faster, simpler approval
Secured loan
  • Backed by property or assets
  • Assets at risk on default
  • Usually lower interest rate
Best for

Covering payroll, buying inventory, paying down debt or seizing an opportunity — without putting business or personal assets on the line.
